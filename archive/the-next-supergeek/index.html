The Next SuperGeek

Live events, challenges and a web series celebrating the zany creativity in all things Geek.

Overview

The Next SuperGeek (TNSG) is about discovering the insanity, creativity and overall zany-ness in all things Geek. Beginning with a series of live events and challenges, TNSG is an upcoming web series that pits Geeks of all kinds in showcases and head-to-head battles.
